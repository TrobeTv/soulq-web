2. Who and what this policy covers

This policy applies to users of the SoulQ app and to related web, support and communication processes. By decision of the operator, SoulQ is not provided to persons under 16 years of age. This is the operator’s product age limit; it does not replace the rules of Art. 8 GDPR for cases where a service would be offered to children on the basis of consent.

3. How SoulQ works

SoulQ is a consumer app intended for personal reflection and the support of personal well‑being. It lets you record your subjective mood, sleep quality, related circumstances and optional personal notes, and shows overviews based on your own entries.

According to its current intended purpose, SoulQ is not provided as a medical device. It is not intended for the diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, prediction, prognosis, treatment or alleviation of disease or disability. It does not provide medical, psychological, psychotherapeutic or crisis services.

4. What data we process

4.1 Account and sign‑in

  • e‑mail address, internal account identifier and username;
  • data necessary for authentication, sign‑in method, time of account creation and sign‑in;
  • a record of acceptance of a specific version of the Terms of Use;
  • with Google Sign‑In, data provided by the identity provider to the extent chosen by the user;
  • with Sign in with Apple, data provided during authorisation, including any relay e‑mail address.

4.2 Profile

  • year of birth;
  • optionally first name, surname, gender and profile photo.

4.3 Personal entries

  • mood score and type, reasons or tags, and date and time;
  • subjective rating of sleep quality and related reasons;
  • an optional personal or journal note;
  • synchronisation metadata necessary to provide the feature.

Personal entries may also contain information concerning physical or mental health if the user enters it into SoulQ themselves. Such data may constitute health data or other special categories of personal data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. SoulQ does not derive a diagnosis from this data and does not assess the user’s health or mental state.

4.4 Security and technical data

  • IP address, time and type of authentication event;
  • security and operational metadata necessary to protect the account and the service;
  • data contained in support requests, rights requests and feedback.

6. Explicit consent for special categories

Before the first processing of personal entries that may, depending on their content, constitute special categories of personal data, SoulQ obtains separate explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. This consent is not part of accepting the Terms of Use and is not pre‑ticked.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time directly in the app under Profile → Settings → Privacy → Withdraw consent or by e‑mail to info@soulq.cz. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal. After withdrawal, further processing of personal data based on this consent stops without undue delay. Further handling of already stored data is governed by section 13 of this policy.

7. Automated decision‑making and AI

According to the available technical documentation, the current version does not send personal entries to an external artificial‑intelligence service. Overviews are generated by deterministic logic on the device. SoulQ does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

8. Recipients and providers

  • Supabase: cloud PostgreSQL database, authentication and Storage in the Frankfurt region, eu‑central‑1.
  • Google and Apple: only for the chosen sign‑in method, to the extent of authentication.
  • Resend: a service used for the technical delivery of beta‑feedback messages to the SoulQ mailbox, where this feature is active.
  • Google Workspace: the mailboxes info@soulq.cz and admin@soulq.cz.

These providers gain access to personal data only to the extent necessary to provide the respective services. Their own processing of personal data may also be governed by their terms and privacy policies.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some service providers or their sub‑processors may also process personal data outside the European Economic Area. In that case we will ensure that the transfer takes place in accordance with Chapter V GDPR, in particular on the basis of a European Commission adequacy decision, the EU‑US Data Privacy Framework for recipients covered by it, or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, where applicable together with further measures required by applicable law.

10. Storage and security

SoulQ uses appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. Server data is stored via Supabase, with the main SoulQ database project operated in the Frankfurt region (eu‑central‑1). Access to stored data is governed by access rules, and profile photos are stored in a non‑public bucket. In the supported version, the app’s local database is encrypted with SQLCipher using the device’s secure key storage. We review and update security measures on an ongoing basis with regard to the nature of the processed data and the related risks.

12. Deleting the account and individual data

The user can delete the account at any time directly in the app via Profile → Settings → Delete account. Account deletion is irreversible. Merely signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete the account. Alternatively, deletion can be requested at info@soulq.cz from the e‑mail address linked to the account. A step‑by‑step guide is on the Delete account page.

On account deletion we remove the account, profile data, personal entries, mood and sleep data, personal notes, the profile photo and related synchronisation data in accordance with this policy and the stated retention rules. Some limited technical or legal records may be retained for a defined period where their further retention is necessary for securing the service, documenting legal acts or complying with a legal obligation. Details of retention periods are set out in section 11 of this policy.

13. Effect of withdrawing Art. 9 consent

After explicit consent is withdrawn, SoulQ stops further processing of personal data based on that consent. App features that require such processing cannot be used for as long as consent is not given.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal. The user can remove their data at any time through the app’s features or delete the entire account via Profile → Settings → Delete account. Where further retention of some data is necessary on another legal ground, it will be retained only to the necessary extent and for the defined period.

14. User rights

The user has the right to:

  • access their personal data,
  • rectification of inaccurate data,
  • erasure under the conditions of Art. 17 GDPR,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability for data processed by automated means on the basis of a contract or consent,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent,
  • lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection or another competent supervisory authority.

Requests can be submitted to info@soulq.cz. We will respond without undue delay, generally within one month at the latest; under the conditions of the GDPR this period may be extended by a further two months.

15. Children

By decision of the operator, SoulQ is not provided to persons under 16 years of age and is neither designed for nor marketed to them. If we have reasonable grounds to find that an account is used by a person under 16, we will carry out appropriate verification and take appropriate steps to terminate the account and erase the personal data, unless other legal grounds for its further processing exist.

16. Changes to this policy

We will inform users appropriately of material changes, in particular a new purpose, provider, AI, health feature, advertising or a new type of data. If a change requires new consent, we will ask for it before the respective processing begins. The current version will be available in the app and on the official website soulq.cz.

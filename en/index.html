The SoulQ story

How the idea for SoulQ was born.

A personal story from the founder about why SoulQ exists at all.

The inspiration for SoulQ was my daughter, who is on the autism spectrum.

From an early age I looked for ways to help her understand the world around her better. Not because she wasn't smart. Quite the opposite. But some connections that most people pick up on automatically simply weren't visible to her.

  • Why do I feel bad today?
  • Did something that happened this morning affect me? Yesterday? Last week?
  • Why am I irritable?
  • Why am I happy?

Over time I realised this isn't just her story. In truth, most of us struggle with the same thing.

Every day we go through dozens of situations, relationships, decisions and emotions. Some things shape us, some we shape ourselves. Yet we often don't see these connections. We notice individual moments, but the bigger picture escapes us.

And that's when the idea for SoulQ was born.

A place that helps you pause, look at your life from a distance and start discovering connections that would otherwise stay hidden. A personal compass. A guide that helps you better understand yourself, your emotions, habits, relationships and decisions. Because I believe that when you understand yourself, you can understand your life better too.

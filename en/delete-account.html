You can permanently delete your SoulQ account, including all data, at any time. There are two ways:

1. Directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the SoulQ app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate, without a quarantine period, and irreversible. Merely signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete the account.

2. Request by email

If you cannot sign in to the app, send a deletion request from the email address you registered with to info@soulq.cz with the subject "SoulQ — account deletion request". We will process the request without undue delay, generally within one month at the latest, and confirm it to you.

3. What will be deleted

  • your user account (e‑mail, username) and profile data (year of birth and, if provided, first name, surname and gender),
  • all your personal entries — mood, sleep quality, their reasons and notes,
  • your profile photo,
  • the link to Google or Apple sign‑in,
  • related synchronisation data.

4. Retention after deletion

Deletion is permanent and irreversible; data is removed from the production database immediately. Only limited technical or legal records may be retained for a defined period where this is necessary for securing the service, documenting legal acts or complying with a legal obligation — in particular:

  • security and authentication logs for no more than 90 days,
  • feedback given on account deletion for no more than 12 months, unless irreversibly anonymised earlier,
  • a minimal record of acceptance of the Terms of Use and of giving or withdrawing explicit consent for 3 years after the account ends.

The full overview of retention periods is in the Privacy Policy, sections 11 and 12.

Tip: The local (offline) copy of your data on the device can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your system settings. Uninstalling alone does not delete the account on the server — use one of the procedures above.
